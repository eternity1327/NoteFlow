## AI inside the product

NoteFlow's agent is rule-based. It uses MeTTa (OpenCog Hyperon) to score ideas and learn from feedback. Chords, structures and lyric sketches come from built-in music theory rules and word lists. It does not call any external AI model or API, and it does not use any partner data. In the split sheet, the AI's suggestions are disclosed and no royalty share is assigned to the AI.

# 3-minute video script

Record the screen with sound on so judges hear the chords. Ask before filming anyone's face, and again before publishing.

Before recording: stop the app, delete the `data` folder, and start it again so the demo begins fresh.

## 0:00 to 0:20, the problem

"Musicians struggle to grow ideas in their own style, and co-writers rarely write down who did what. NoteFlow is a MeTTa co-writer that starts small, learns your taste, shows exactly how it changed, and drafts the split sheet."

## 0:20 to 0:45, preferences become rules

Open **Preferences**. Type your name, pick a genre, a mood and a tempo. Click **Save & calibrate agent**.

"MeTTa turns my preferences into just seven starting rules."

Point at the starting rule set, then show `metta/hints.metta` for 5 seconds.

## 0:45 to 1:45, it grows up

In the **Studio**, play the idea.

"It wrote 12 ideas and MeTTa scored them. Here is why it picked this one."

Point at **Why this idea**. Click **+ Darker chords**.

"Watch: the next idea is in a minor key, and it says the darker request shaped it."

Type "make it jazzier and faster" and send. Point at the 9th chords and the new tempo. Click **Keep for song**. Change the Musician name to a teammate and keep another idea.

Open **Engine**. "Seven rules grew to N. Every card shows the feedback, the idea before, the idea after, and each weight change. Here is the full MeTTa diff since setup."

## 1:45 to 2:30, the split sheet

Open **Split sheet**. Add a song title. Both musicians who kept ideas are already listed. Add a lyricist. Tick roles, type each person's share and click **Check with MeTTa**. Tick **Agreed** for everyone.

"The collaborators set the shares, MeTTa checks they add up to 100%, and the sheet stays a draft until everyone agrees. The AI is disclosed and gets no share."

Click **Download** and show the file.

## 2:30 to 3:00, what's next

"Next we want it to learn combinations and melodies, export MIDI and stems, keep one profile per musician, and send signed splits to Incantio once the partner confirms. Thanks from Gerard, Rufert and Michael."
